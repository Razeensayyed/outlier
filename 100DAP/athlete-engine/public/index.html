Built by a competitive athlete.
Not a content creator.

Kunal Gaikwad is a competitive athlete and conditioning coach who has spent years studying the gap between gym performance and field performance. He's worked with people who are strong, disciplined, and consistent — and still gas out in 10 minutes.

Athlete Engine is the exact system he uses with athletes who need to perform for 90 minutes, not just 10 reps. It is not a generic training plan. It is a structured 100-day progression designed to develop your aerobic base from the ground up, build lactate threshold, and convert that endurance into explosive sport-ready power.

6 sessions a week. 1 hour per session. One program, built to take you from gassing out to being the last one standing.

"The athletes I've worked with didn't need more strength. They needed to build the engine that would make their strength last."
