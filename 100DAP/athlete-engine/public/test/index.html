How your score works

  • Each station earns 4, 8, 12, 16 or 20 points based on your result. A run you did not finish scores 0.
  • Total score = Plank + Squats + Push-ups + Burpees + Run, out of 100.
  • Levels: 0–39 Gym Fit · 40–59 Conditioned · 60–79 Hybrid · 80–100 Athlete.
  • Your weakest link is the station with the lowest points. Retest on Day 100 to see how far you've come.
